Introduction to Watcher Divination

     When working with these particular spirits and sigils one must be aware
of their own freedom of choice and logic almost never going to extremes in
thinking or reckless behavior. Please keep in mind that it is necessary for
you- if you are evoking or calling these spirits into your space-to have a
strong connection to your instincts or at least be cautious when having
direct contact with them. Their presence is in most cases very intense and
can interrupt your daily routine i.e. sleep etc. That being said they are
indeed some of the most chaotically thrilling beings you can call into your
life. If you're looking for adventure or to just shake up your mundane life
and routines and create new and exciting (not to mention often scary)
changes, opportunities and all around growth through trials and being
pushed to new and better manifestations of  who you are as a person then by
all means delve deeply into the work with caution.
     I emphasize the word caution here as it is necessary, and part of being
"cautious" is being  fearless in working with the Angelic Watchers…by
their very nature if you are overly fearful of them they as predatory spirits
may decide to feed on you or destroy you from the inside out if they are so
inclined. It is a necessary requisite in my opinion as a Left Hand Path Male
Witch to have a good handle on your fear of spirits specifically. If you have
any experience with energy work then I am probably preaching to the choir;
for example an everyday astral intrusive energy (for me one would usually
appear in the form of a rat in my mental eyes.) senses the fear or other such
intense negative emotion and go's to its victim attaches itself then uses that
fear thought as a tool by causing it to intensify in the victim's mind and aura
field. this creates more energy for its feeding meanwhile the victim grows
weaker and more anxious to the point of needing outside help.
     My point is not to compare the Watchers to an everyday intrusive energy
but rather to emphasize the reality that being overly fearful is like
announcing your vulnerability to anything that is predatory whether an
animal, insect (e.g. wasps, hornets) or even other humans. People who may
dislike you or are actively envious of you and see you as an easy target;
weak and vulnerable. Spirits are no different if they for some reason or
another do not like you and sense your weaknesses…guilt, fear, etc…they
can like any human or animal see you as their prey. They can easily

penetrate your astral barriers into your inner aura if fear is present. Like a
window to your soul that is not at all something you want in this case.
     Keep in mind also that the pervading emotion of fear may be present
during your evocations and workings with these kind of spirits but it may
not be your own if you have any empathic capacity a spirit may induce
feelings of fear into you causing your actual fear reflex to kick in…tricky
devils. If this happens don’t let it escalate just know that it is not real and
that you must keep your presence of mind…often it is a little test they will
impress on you I have found in my own experiences, however each
individual's experience is different I'm sure. It may be that you are a very
experienced magickian that has no fear or concern with your level of
psychic defense. If so then I don’t imagine working with the Watchers is
much of a road block for you. I do still recommend a good amount of
respect and to avoid an attitude of overconfidence.
     I am in no way trying to dissuade you from using this tool/psychic lever
to better yourself or your life whether neophyte or advanced magikian. I
simply need to be honest and open and put all the cards on the table…pun
intended, to absolve myself from any guilt I may potentially feel if you the
reader piss off the wrong spirit.
     And of course working directly with the Watchers is not really necessary
when using this sigil set but it can be beneficial for those who are prepared
for such intense self work.

Creation of the Sigils of Desire

     Originally I created most of these specific sigils for the purpose of
calling forth the ideas or desires into my life through traditional (if you
want to call it that) sigil charging means. If you are not familiar with what
those are I will explain the basic premise of it.
     One may want or need a boost of willpower, or even physical or mental
endurance and strength, in which case one would channel a sigil or create a
sigil in whatever myriad of ways that are available to them then precede to
consciously forget the meaning of it and absorb it into their mind…the
unconscious mind specifically. In order for this to happen one usually needs
to disconnect or shutdown deliberate non-instinctive thought that blocks
access to unconscious levels of mind. One way is the sexual orgasm for
example.
     At the climax one would gaze at the sigil without thinking which is easy
when your absorbed and enveloped by pleasure. This is one way how the
unconscious realms are accessed.  This is just one example of absorbing a
sigil…there are many other ways. It really depends on the individual as to
what will work best in my opinion.
     I created combinations of these sigils for specific purposes and charged
them or absorbed them in order to bring my wishes into physical reality and
others I made exclusively for the divination set that follows in the pages to
come.
     The set of sigils seemed to come together purely by instinct or feel…not
to say that I sloppily pieced together this set of sigils and their meanings but
rather the meanings themselves are a culmination of my own life
experiences, failures, trials, tragedies, and hard lessons learned. They are
practical to apply to everyday life which is often an inner struggle more so
than an outside issue for people that I have met and for myself indeed this is
also true. Some of the meanings naturally revolve around the life of the
Witch or Magickian of the Left Hand Path specifically, but also does not
necessarily exclude those who are inclined to work toward ascension or
dissolution of self into the void to end their cycle of life in the flesh.
     Additionally I want to encourage you to add your own sigils to the set.
And if you're so inclined write down what each sigil given in the material
means to you, especially the sigils of the Watchers which are more open to

interpretation or one's own experience with each Watcher should they evoke
them themselves.
     In this grimoire the sigil meanings of the Watchers are based partly on
their attributes written by Michael W. Ford and a lot of credit goes to him
for his excellent book Adversarial Light. Also I want to point out that if you
decide to do more work with the Watchers then his book is a suitable and
more than adequate grimoire/manual for that purpose.
     And of course I do emphasize the need to understand how to read cards
in general assuming you use the sigils on cards. A good card reader knows
that instinct/intuition guides you to the meaning of each spread. Each
written meaning is simply a general guidepost pointing in the right direction
when surrounding cards are considered and taken into account.

Suggestions On How To Make And Consecrate The Sigil Set

     The sigils are generally best applied and or inscribed with ink (mixed
with a drop of the users blood to help create an energetic connection to
them if you desire) on a blank tarot deck but it is really up to you how you
want to use them. You may want to use them rune style and inscribe them
on wooden discs with a wood burner for example if you are skilled at it. I
recommend using the blank cards and a pen and ink, markers are also
acceptable.
     I do recommend the use of a small drop of blood on the outer edge of the
deck of cards when consecrating or in the ink itself. The cards will work
with or without this, however I'm not going to bullshit you and say that
blood does not make them stronger and better tools…especially with the
understanding of the Watchers as predatory spirits.
    After the cards or whatever type of set you've created is done and ready
for use please take the time to dedicate/ consecrate the cards appropriately
for best results. Here I am providing a general consecration evocation of the
Watchers associated with divination type practice and tarot.
     The first Watcher associated with the practice of divination is Rameel.
Rameel is a serpent/dragon like being with scaly skin. He hisses and moves
about swiftly like he is about to strike. He circles about the chamber and
will give you information very directly in an urgent voice…a voice of harsh
and cutting intonation.
     Remember when working with Rameel that his words are advice only,
even when he sounds like he is commanding you to do this or that please
use your logic and discretion to avoid pitfalls. I suggest that you do not
disrespect this powerful being in any way. He conversely will probably not
respect you if you do every little thing he says without thinking for
yourself.
     The Watchers are indeed spirits of the Adversary and generally speaking
they have little regard for the stupid and easily fooled individual that is at
their beck and call in every respect. Use your logic and senses wisely and
remember to take a balanced approach to this work.
     Kokabel is the second Watcher whom you will be evoking for the
consecration of this sigil set. He has an intense presence and speaks in a

normal male voice. He has no specific form but seems to take many
different forms during an evocation. In my experience he seemed insect like
mostly. He had a sort of human praying mantis type shape that seemed to
last longer than the other anthropomorphic forms he presented.
     This evocation of the two aforementioned Watchers is meant only as a
suggestion or recommendation on how the sigil set should be consecrated. I
would not attempt this evocation of the Watchers if you are not familiar
with magical practice and how to handle and work with them.
     I will not be outlining every detail of how evokation works as this is not
a grimoire/system of magical practice, but rather of the specific practice of
sigil divination.
     For those that do not have experience with Magick or Witchcraft I am
providing a simple and general dedication/consecration statement that may
be recited while focusing on the sigil set you create.

them over the sigil set.

your energies blend with the sigils out of your right hand. Visualize a

to man the light of divination. In the name of Rameel and Kokabel I bless
these sigils to be a light to my path and beyond into the unseen depths of my
inner life for myself and those whom I wish to aid. I thank now the ancients
ones for their blessing upon me.

sigils work well after the present position and before the future position I
find. Again, it's up to you how you use the sigils. I recommend
experimenting to find what works best.

Meanings For The Sigils of Desire

1. Ace:
Element-Earth: This particular Sigil of Desire conveys the idea of
perfection and /or a high level of mastery in ones arena of activities. It
indicates that you are a true master of controlling the inclination to indulge
in weak minded pleasures but instead opt for lasting gratification of a more
refined and hard to come by variety…eloquence and making the most
difficult of tasks appear to be easy is indicated. Great patience and vision of
a grand outcome are also shown with this most potent sigils. The giving up
of short term pleasures for long term reward and empowerment.
Inverse Meaning: Impatience and an attitude of "how hard I've been
working" that is overemphasized in your daily interactions…no vision of
the long term goals that need to be patiently chipped away at in order to be
fully enfleshed and realized.

2. In Dream:
Element-Water: This sigil is an indication that messages from the spirits
you work with will come through in dreams tonight. It may also indicate
that you recently had an intense dream and to try to understand the
symbolism of it because it is important for you to see. In some instances it
also shows that a message is coming to you presently through intuition or
even telepathically from spirit…listen closely.
Inverse Meaning:
If pulled inverse it indicates that one needs to be more grounded in their
daily lives and find a more realistic outlook on a situation that they may be
facing.

3. Ease:
Element-water: This Sigil of Desire is self explanatory for the most part, it
is a signpost of allowing a thing to come into being rather than using force
to bring it forward. Let go and it will come with little if any effort…relax
into the process. Also indicates the task or obstacle you are facing is not as
worthy of the amount of anxiety you may be allowing yourself to feel.
Inverse Meaning: Difficulty in completing a task and or achieving a goal.
Tension and anxiety that is stunting the growth process or flow of creative
energy. Let go of the outcome and focus on doing your best one day at a
time.

4. Enjoyment:
Element-Air and Water: This sigil is self explanatory it shows that feelings
of joy, excitement and happiness are felt or will be felt and experienced.
Inverse Meaning: In the inverse aspect the sigil indicates feelings of
sadness and struggling to regain a positive outlook and lightheartedness.
should you find yourself in this predicament remember that it is a sadness
that can be corrected; it is a motivator for one to desire change and growth.

5. Honesty:
Element-Water: When this sigil is drawn it indicates that more honesty is
called for or it can mean that the person whom you may suspect is lying is
being truthful. Overall a positive card as it indicates that the one whom you
need to be honest with will be open and receptive to what you have to say.
Inverse Meaning: If pulled inverse…it is a warning to take heed as to what
you are revealing to others. It could be that you are talking to much about
your future plans when they are still in the stages of infancy and need to be
kept private so they can have a chance to manifest without the thoughts and
opinions of others interfering or altering the outcome or growth.

6. To Invoke:
Element-Water: This card is a reminder of the powerful, primal forces
within you as an evolved being. It tells you that you are a dynamo of
untapped subconscious sources of motivation and strength. If pulled as an
answer to a situation it indicates that you must rely on your primal instincts
that drive you to survive no matter what you’re facing. It indicates that you
have the tools you need to overcome any obstacle or challenge. For
magicians it can also indicate using sorcery as a means to manifest your
True Will in this world. Use the power of the unconscious mind to change
your circumstance or even your inner state.
Inverse Meaning: You are having trouble controlling your lusts and desires
and it is affecting your life negatively. You must learn control or face de-
compensation of Self, learn to uproot the inner discord that is causing your
lack of discipline. It may be as simple as an emotional deficit, a lack of
connection to community and friends or even depression of some kind.
Indicates one needs to learn to focus and concentrate more. Additionally it
is a reminder to go with the ebb and flow cycles…regulate your
indulgences and be gentle with yourself.

7. Meditation:
Element- Spirit: This sigil implies that one needs to use the tool meditation
in their situation or it could mean that one’s mind and spirit are healthy and
balanced through meditative practices. Also that one has achieved a
connection or conversation/communion with their Daemon or Holy
Guardian Angel.
Inverse Meaning:
One has lost touch with their True Will and lost connection with the
lifeblood of their purpose. They are anxious and unable to focus because of
addictions and being easily distracted by pleasures or unnecessary drama
that is not healthy for the person. It could be that there is a person or clique
that is holding one back.

8. The Sabbat:

Element-Air and Earth: Should you pull this symbol it may indicate that
you will be well served to seek out people with similar interests and ideas
whether romantically or just making new friends. Surrounding cards should
tell which. For Witches or Magikians it may also be   a reminder for them to
seek out others on the astral or physical plain that practice the Path of The
Wise or whatever path you may follow.
Inverse Meaning:
Take some time to find solitude and inner peace and/or reflection. Avoid
large crowds at this time and do more introverted activities.

9. Speed:
Element-Fire: Simply indicates that the events in question have, are going
to, or are currently unfolding very quickly. Also can show that speed or an
attitude of urgency is needed in the situation.
Inverse Meaning: The simple yet very important message of "slow down"
is stated by the inverse meaning of this sigil. May also show that events are
moving slowly.

10. Strength:
Element-Water: Inner reserves of grueling toughness are to be called on in
the situation and often this includes a great amount of endurance as this is
obviously one of the most important attributes of strength. Do not ever give
up on yourself. This is a positive card in that it shows the strength is within
you and you are using it well. Remember that the strongest of us are
adaptable like water, you sense the currents and moods of life and maneuver
yourself into the right moment and position to dissolve obstacles.
Inverse Meaning: Lack of patients and endurance…wishful for results
when you have not done the work and created the immovable foundation of
trial and error. In the inverse meaning patients is called for to chip away at
the obstacle or task…be tenacious and endure the tedium.

11. Take Flight:
Element-Air and Fire: When one pulls this sigil it is an indication that you
are to flee the situation or battle that is in question that you may be facing.
Better to conserve strength and fight another day or it may be a battle that
may not necessarily ever have to be…
Inverse Meaning: The time to face your conflict is now, to run would be a
mistake and lead to more troubles…face the challenge whatever it may be.

12. Transformation:
Element-Fire: This Sigil of Desire is a message that the time is at hand in
which you must exit you're cocoon and be free of constraints that are no
longer needed. These constraints may be self-imposed or someone who may
be protecting you. Remove the training wheels and be the colorfully unique
person you know you are inside as the amount of protection and or
sheltering is no longer serving your highest good and authentic self.
Inverse Meaning: In the inverse position most likely you may be very
eager to jump into the fray of that new life phase or job etc. but the message
is one of needing to grow a bit stronger and/or become your ideal self first
or you may become confused and be weakened by misguidance or those
that surround you, pulling you into their way of thinking. Allow your new
self to crystallize to the core before moving forward.

13.This My Wish:
Element-Spirit: This sigil is a calling to the person for whom it is pulled to
set intentions of manifesting his or her desires into being, whether material
desires or other. It can indicate that one has abundance and that they have
the support of spirit to have their wishes granted within reason.
Inverse Meaning:
This acts has a heads up that something in your life will come to end to

# Gematria & Numberology App — ARITHMOS HO THEOS ("Numbers Are God")

An interactive Gematria, Isopsephy, and Sacred Numerology web application that ciphers any given word, name, verse, or phrase into numbers and explains its esoteric and mathematical meaning across ancient traditions.

## Features

- **Complete 28-Cipher GematriaCalculator.us Suite**:
  - **Base & Reduction (9)**: English Ordinal, Full Reduction, Reverse Ordinal, Reverse Full Reduction, Single Reduction, Full Reduction KV, Single Reduction KV, Reverse Single Reduction, Reverse Full Reduction EP.
  - **Jewish & Kabbalah (7)**: Jewish (Agrippa Latin), Jewish Reduction, Jewish Ordinal, Alphanumeric Qabbalah (AQ Base-36), English Extended, Sumerian, Reverse Sumerian.
  - **Esoteric, Occult & Baconian (8)**: Satanic, Reverse Satanic, Septenary, Chaldean, Francis Bacon (Case-Sensitive), Franc Baconis (Case-Sensitive), Reverse Francis Bacon, Keypad.
  - **Mathematical Sequences (4)**: Primes, Trigonal (Triangular Numbers), Squares, Fibonacci.
- **Interactive Cipher Inspector**: Click any cipher card to inspect the exact letter-by-letter sum equation and the full A–Z cipher key chart.
- **7 Sacred Knowledge Traditions**:
  1. **Universal Synthesis ("Numbers Are God")** & Sacred Concordance
  2. **Jewish Kabbalah, Sefer Yetzirah & The 10 Sefirot** (Interactive Tree of Life SVG + Mispar Hechrachi, Mispar Gadol, Mispar Siduri, and Atbash `אתב"ש`)
  3. **Biblical Numerology & Scriptural Factor Decomposition**
  4. **Greek Isopsephy (`Ἰσοψηφία`) & Pythagorean Arithmology** (Interactive Sacred Tetractys SVG)
  5. **Astrology & Cornelius Agrippa's Planetary Kameas** (Live Magic Square Generator for Saturn, Jupiter, Mars, Sun, Venus, Mercury, Moon)
  6. **Druidic Ogham Tree Oracle (`Beith-Luis-Nin`) & Celtic Triads (`Awen /|\`)**
  7. **Harmonic Synastry Comparator** (Compare any two words or names side-by-side)

## Usage

Open `index.html` in any modern web browser. No build step or external server required.
